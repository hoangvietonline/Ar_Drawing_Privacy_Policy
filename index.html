Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you draw, capture, save, and share with AR Drawing Sketchar.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

At a glance

  • Your drawing content is processed on your device.
  • No app account is required. The App includes Google advertising and may offer optional Premium subscriptions.
  • Google services handle technical data and downloads. Device backup may include app data.
  • You control camera permission, exported files, and sharing.

The sections below explain these practices in detail.

01 Who we are and what this policy covers

KhoaiStore ("we," "us," or "our") provides AR Drawing Sketchar (the "App"), an Android drawing and tracing app. This policy covers the App, including package com.khoaidev.ardrawingsketchar, and this privacy policy website.

We do not operate an App account system or a server that receives your drawings, camera feed, imported photos, or recorded videos. Some information is handled by Google services, Android backup, services you choose for sharing, or our support email, as explained below.

02 Information the App handles

Camera, photos, and videos

The App uses the camera for live drawing guidance, taking photos, and recording videos when you use those features. You can select an image through Android's system picker for use as a drawing reference. The App may copy selected images into its local cache and process them for cropping, rotation, background removal, or photo comparisons.

These operations take place on your device. We do not upload this media to a developer-operated server. Video recording in the current App does not record microphone audio. Images you select may contain information or metadata supplied by their source; sharing or exporting a file can include information contained in that file.

Drawing projects and local progress

The App stores drawing projects, favorites, completed drawing sessions, drawing duration, template references, album references, and feature preferences in app storage on your device. This information supports saving work, your collection, and progress displays. The profile area is a local collection, not an online account.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to provide. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Our email service provider also processes the correspondence to deliver and store it.

Technical information

Google components used by the App handle technical information as described in Section 4, including advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Local drawing progress is distinct from information collected by advertising services.

03 Permissions and your choices

  • Camera: used for camera-based tracing, photos, and video capture. You can deny or revoke access in Android Settings; camera features will then be unavailable.
  • Selected images: the system picker lets you choose which image to open. The App does not request broad photo-library read permission.
  • Storage on Android 9 and earlier: the App may request storage permission to save exported media to shared storage. Newer Android versions use system media APIs for this purpose.

The current App does not request microphone, contacts, or device-location permissions. Denying a permission may limit the feature that needs it. You can manage permissions in Android Settings → Apps → AR Drawing Sketchar → Permissions; labels may vary by device.

04 Google services and device backup

Google AdMob advertising and privacy choices

The App includes Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When advertising services run, Google and participating advertising providers may collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app and device details, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostic information. These data support ad delivery, personalization where permitted, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The App does not provide your drawings, imported images, camera feed, or recorded videos to advertising services.

Google's consent-management service handles information needed to determine which privacy message applies and stores your choices. Where required, you will be asked for consent before relevant advertising processing. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is displayed, and manage advertising identifiers through Android privacy settings. Declining personalization does not necessarily stop all ads or all technical data processing.

Advertising recipients depend on the ad providers enabled for the App. Where a privacy message applies, it provides vendor information and choices. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Google Play Billing: optional Premium purchases

If you purchase Premium when available, Google Play processes the payment using your Google account. We do not receive your full payment-card details. The App and its billing component process purchase information, such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and subscription entitlement, to verify purchases and provide paid access. Purchase and entitlement information may be stored locally. Google retains its transaction records under its own policies and legal obligations. Clearing App data does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Google ML Kit: background removal

The App uses Google's ML Kit subject segmentation. Images and segmentation results are processed on-device and are not sent to Google by ML Kit. Google may download or update the required model.

ML Kit collects device and app details, device or installation identifiers, performance measurements, configuration, input/output sizes, feature versions, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this technical data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Learn more: ML Kit privacy information, ML Kit data disclosures, and Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Asset Delivery: drawing templates

The App downloads additional drawing template and lesson packs through Google Play Asset Delivery. Google processes device metadata and the App version to deliver compatible packs and maintain them after updates. Google documents encrypted transfer and retention under its fixed retention periods.

Learn more: Play Asset Delivery data handling and Google Privacy Policy.

Android backup and gallery sync

The App allows Android backup and device transfer. Depending on your device and backup settings, eligible app data, including projects, preferences, progress, and media in app storage, may be backed up by your system backup provider or transferred to another device. We do not receive or control those backups.

Photos and videos you export to your gallery may also be synced by your chosen gallery or cloud service. Manage backup, restore, and cloud copies through your device or service settings. Clearing local App data does not necessarily delete existing cloud backups or synced gallery files. See Android backup information.

05 Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your drawings or use your drawing content for advertising. The App does not automatically publish your drawings or media. Advertising services receive technical and interaction data as described in Section 4; depending on applicable law, some advertising disclosures may be treated as sharing for targeted advertising. You may exercise applicable choices through the advertising privacy controls or contact us for assistance.

When you use a sharing feature, Android lets you choose a destination app or service and grants it access to the selected file. That destination handles the file under its own policies. Information may also be handled by the providers described in this policy to deliver their services.

If we hold information from a support request, we may disclose it when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or as reasonably necessary to protect users, security, or legal rights.

06 Retention and deletion

  • App data: projects, progress, preferences, and local references remain until removed through available App controls, cleared in Android Settings, or removed when you uninstall the App. Some temporary files are cleaned up by the App or Android; no single automatic deletion period applies to all local data.
  • Exported media: photos and videos saved to shared storage can remain after uninstalling. Delete them using your gallery or file manager. Removing an album reference inside the App does not necessarily delete the exported file.
  • Shared files and backups: manage or request deletion through the destination service or backup provider. Deleting a local file does not recall copies already shared or backed up.
  • Support correspondence: we keep messages for as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request and related issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the contact below.
  • Purchase records: local entitlement and purchase records may remain until App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled, and may be restored from Google Play when purchases are checked again. Google Play retains transaction records separately. Support records related to a purchase may be retained to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.
  • Google technical and advertising data: Google manages retention and deletion under its applicable policies. We do not set a separate retention period for data held by Google.

To reset local App data, open Android Settings → Apps → AR Drawing Sketchar → Storage → Clear storage/data. This removes your local work and settings. Because the App has no online accounts, no App account deletion process is needed.

07 Security and international processing

App-private files use Android's app storage protections. Exported and shared files are accessible according to the permissions and settings of the destinations you choose. Protect your device and use care when sharing images that contain personal information.

Google and other service providers described above may process information in countries other than yours, under their respective policies. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

08 Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information necessary to deliver requested services and purchases to perform our agreement with you, rely on consent for processing that requires it (including relevant advertising processing), and rely on legitimate interests for proportionate support, security, and fraud prevention where those interests are not overridden by your rights. We also process information to meet legal obligations. A device permission or acceptance of our Terms does not replace legally required advertising consent.

You can control local content and permissions as described above. For information you have sent directly to us, contact us to exercise an applicable right. We may need enough information to verify your request. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete files stored only on your device, and requests concerning information held independently by Google or another provider should also be directed to that provider.

09 Children's privacy

The App does not ask children to provide a name, email address, or an online profile. Parents and guardians should supervise camera access, image selection, and sharing, and use device controls appropriate for their child. Technical processing by Google services, including advertising, and system backups is described above. Parental supervision does not replace any consent or protections required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us without appropriate parental permission, contact us so we can review the matter and delete that information where required.
